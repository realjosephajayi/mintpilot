Automatic NFT minting

Pick a collection, set a time, and MintPilot signs and sends the mint the moment it opens. No waiting up, no missed drops.

Open MintPilot in Telegram
1. Add funds

Send ETH to your MintPilot wallet on the network you want to mint on.

2. Schedule

Choose the collection, how many to mint and when.

3. Relax

MintPilot mints for you and messages you the result.

MintPilot holds your wallet key so it can sign for you. Only add funds you can afford to lose. Never share a recovery phrase with anyone.
